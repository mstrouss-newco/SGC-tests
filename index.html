A few things you might want to know.

About the fund

What kinds of expenses can you help with?

Essential payments where falling behind causes lasting damage. Today that means mortgage payments, car payments, and important bills that have fallen behind. What fits depends on the situation and the obligation itself.

Can you cover more than one payment?

Yes. Where it makes the difference between steadying and slipping further, we can cover the same obligation for several months, for example a mortgage or a car payment over six months, rather than patching a single month.

Can assistance ever be cash?

Sometimes. We prioritize paying the obligation itself, because that is usually what solves the problem. In emergencies and disaster relief, getting money to someone quickly can be the more effective way to help.

If you give

Where does my gift go?

Into the Covered Fund alongside gifts from the rest of the community, then out as assistance to people who qualify. Pooling gifts lets help go where it does the most good rather than tying one gift to one person.

Is my gift tax deductible?

Yes. Gifts are received by Our Change Foundation, a 501(c)(3), and your receipt is emailed to you.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Anytime, in one click from any receipt.

Does the host choose who gets help?

No. Hosts help raise the money. They never review applications, choose who receives help, or see anyone’s financial information. Every application goes through the same review.

If you need help

How do you verify someone actually needs help?

With their permission, we use secure technology similar to what banks use to confirm identity, review their financial profile, and check that the need is real. That way decisions rest on real information rather than on who tells the best story.

Will my story be shared if I receive help?

We report back to donors about what their giving accomplished. That can include the expense covered, the amount of assistance, the circumstances and the outcome, without identifying you.

Your name, photo, video and any other identifying details are only ever shared if you agree to take part.

Need help with a payment?

If you have fallen behind on something essential, ask us. It takes a few minutes and there is no fundraiser to build and no audience to convince.

  • You live in the United States and you are 18 or older.
  • You are behind, or about to fall behind, on a mortgage, a car payment or another important bill.
  • With your permission, we confirm your identity, look at your financial profile and check the need.
  • Where we can, we pay the mortgage servicer, the lender or the utility directly. In an emergency, we may get money to you instead.
