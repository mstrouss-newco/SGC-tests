The problem

Hardship has a hundred causes. Most happen to people doing everything right.

A job loss. A sudden expense. One difficult month.

Millions of households are one setback away from falling behind. At the same time, the people and organizations that want to help often cannot see where their money went or what it changed.

Standard Giving was built to make help more direct, transparent, and accountable.

How it works

Don't just give. Solve something.

Most giving stops when the money is sent. Standard Giving follows it to a verified expense and pays it directly.

The recipient gets targeted relief. The partner knows exactly where the money went and what it accomplished.

1

You fund it and aim it.

Fund a fixed amount, dedicate a percentage of profits, offer customer round-ups, or rally your audience through crowdfunding. You choose the community you want to support, whether that is your customers, your hometown, or another defined group of people.

2

We verify need and select each recipient.

People apply for support, and our nonprofit verifies financial need using secure open banking technology. The nonprofit independently determines who receives help through a documented and consistent selection process.

3

Verified expenses get paid. You get the story.

We pay approved expenses directly, helping recipients regain stability without adding another layer of uncertainty. You receive clear impact reporting and ready-to-share stories showing exactly what your support accomplished.
