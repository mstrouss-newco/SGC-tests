Questions

Everything you want to know.

Who qualifies?

Any U.S. resident 18 or older who is currently navigating financial hardship. You do not need to be an EarnIn customer, and you do not need to be employed.

Is this a loan?

No. It is a grant. Nothing to repay, no interest, no fees, and no tip of any kind.

How long does the application take?

About two minutes. There is nothing to upload and no documents to gather.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. We use a soft credit check to verify your identity and situation. Soft checks are never reported to lenders as an inquiry and do not change your score.

What financial information is visible?

Only what is needed to confirm the hardship you describe. It is held by Standard Giving, never sold, and never shared with EarnIn.

Does EarnIn see my application?

No. EarnIn is told how much went out and to how many households. It never receives individual applications or applicant details, and applying has no effect on your account.

Is everyone who applies funded?

No. The fund is limited and we are not able to fund every request, or to follow up with every applicant individually. You will always get a confirmation that your application arrived.
